Privacy Policyfor Tggr.

Last updated: 17 July 2026

Tggr runs on infrastructure we own and control directly, rather than a third-party cloud platform. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking — your data is never sold or shared with anyone for marketing.

Who runs this service

Tggr is operated independently (“we”, “us”), with the same care applied to security and privacy as any hosted service. You can reach us at privacy@shreeshasagar.co.in for any question about this policy or your data.

What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to work:

  • Account details from Google Sign-In. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address, and a Google account identifier. We do not receive your Google password, and we cannot access your Gmail, Drive, contacts, or any other Google service.
  • Files you upload. The files themselves, plus their filename, size, and upload time.
  • Tag data. Tags you create (name, description, access setting), which tags you favourite, who owns each tag, and any access requests you send or receive.
  • A session cookie. A single essential cookie that keeps you signed in. See “Cookies” below.

We do not collect analytics, behavioural data, advertising identifiers, or your location. We do not use tracking pixels or third-party analytics scripts of any kind.

Where your data lives

Everything — your account record, your tags, and every file you upload — is stored on infrastructure we operate directly, not on a third-party cloud provider. Account and tag information is kept in a dedicated database alongside the files themselves.

Who can see your files

Access is controlled by the tag a file belongs to:

  • You must be signed in to view or download any file.
  • For tags set to “anyone with the tag name”, any signed-in user who knows that tag name can view and upload files in it. Treat such tag names as shared secrets.
  • For tags set to “by request”, only the tag owner and people the owner has explicitly approved can see the files.
  • Nobody else. Your files are not shared with any third party, and they are not used for advertising, analytics, training, or any purpose other than showing them to the people your tag allows. Administrative access to the infrastructure is limited to those who operate it, and we do not browse, review, or monitor what you upload.

Cookies

We use exactly one cookie: a strictly necessary session cookie (tggr_token) that records that you are signed in. It is httpOnly (not readable by scripts), it is not used for tracking or profiling, and it is removed when you sign out. There are no advertising or analytics cookies, which is why you are not asked for cookie consent.

Third parties

Only two, and neither receives your files or tag data for their own use:

  • Google — provides sign-in. When you use the “Continue with Google” button, Google handles the authentication and tells us your name and email. Their handling of that is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Cloudflare — carries traffic between your browser and our infrastructure, and provides HTTPS encryption. Traffic passes through Cloudflare's network in transit. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with anyone else. We would only disclose data if legally compelled to do so.

How long we keep it

Files and tags are kept until you delete them. Deleting a tag permanently removes the tag and every file inside it from the server. Your account record is kept while your account exists. Note that backups of the server may retain copies for a period after deletion.

Your rights and choices

You can delete your own files and tags at any time from within the app. For anything else — a copy of the data held about you, correction of your details, or complete deletion of your account and its contents — email privacy@shreeshasagar.co.in and we will action it. You can also revoke Tggr's access to your Google account at any time via your Google account permissions page.

Security

Traffic to and from the app is encrypted in transit with HTTPS. Sessions use signed, httpOnly cookies that scripts cannot read, and every single file request is authenticated and checked against your tag permissions before anything is served — there is no way to browse or guess your way to a file you do not have access to.

Your data sits on dedicated infrastructure rather than a shared multi-tenant cloud platform. As with any service, no system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, and this policy does not constitute a warranty.

Children

Tggr is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the “last updated” date above will change with it. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns: privacy@shreeshasagar.co.in
