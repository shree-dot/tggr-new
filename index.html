Tggr

Tggr is a tag-based file sharing app. Create a tag, upload files to it, and share the tag name with anyone you want to collaborate with — no folders, no complicated permissions, just simple, powerful tags to organize and share files across devices in an instant.

  • Organize with tags — group related files together under a single shareable tag.
  • Share in an instant — anyone with the tag name can view or contribute, based on the access rules you choose.
  • Collaborate seamlessly — everyone with access can upload and stay in sync.
