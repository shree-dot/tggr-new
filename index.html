Tggr

Tggr is a tag-based file sharing app. Instead of folders and complicated permissions, you create a tag, upload files to it, and share the tag name with the people you want to collaborate with.

Use Tggr to organize files under tags, share them instantly across devices, and control exactly who can view or upload to each tag. Sign in with your Google account to create your first tag and start sharing.
